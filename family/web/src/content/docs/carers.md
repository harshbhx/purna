# Guide for carers

You make Purna work: you set it up, sit nearby, and keep it gentle. This page is for you, whether you're a son, daughter, partner or paid carer.

## Your part, in short

- **Before:** check [Safety](#safety), clear the table, sit the person down, put the glasses on.
- **During:** stay in the room, stay quiet, and let them search. Watch for any sign of discomfort.
- **After:** take the glasses off, say something kind about the effort (not the score), and rest.

## Agree the plan with the therapist

Ask the therapist, before you start:
- Is home practice right for them now?
- Which level (Easy, Standard or Hard) and which hint delay?
- How often, and for how long?
- What should make you stop and call them?

Write the answers down. Purna will suggest a next level after each session; the therapist's plan comes first.

## Helping without doing it for them

- **Don't point to diyas or say "there's one on the left".** Purna's bell already points to the side. Finding them is the practice.
- If they're upset, stop. A short session that ends well is better than a long one that doesn't.
- Praise the trying, not the numbers. Some days will be better than others, and that's normal.

## Seeing progress

On Purna Family you'll see one sentence per session and a trend over time. The shaded band on the trend shows the usual day-to-day change: **a change inside the band may just be chance**, so look at weeks, not single days. Bring questions to the therapist rather than drawing conclusions yourself.

## Sharing with the therapist

On the dashboard, **Share with doctor** makes a read-only link that works for 7 days. Send it to the therapist. You can stop it at any time with **Stop sharing**. The therapist can leave you a note, which appears on your dashboard. They can also **set the next session**: the glasses then suggest that level and hint delay on the setup panel, and your dashboard says so. You can still change it if the day calls for it.

## Looking after yourself

Caring is tiring. Purna is meant to take a few minutes, not add a chore. If it stops helping or starts causing stress for either of you, it's fine to pause and talk to the therapist.

## Your privacy controls

- **Sign out here** forgets the family link on this device only.
- **Delete everything** removes every session, note, doctor link and the paired glasses. It can't be undone.

See [Privacy](#privacy) for what's stored and for how long.

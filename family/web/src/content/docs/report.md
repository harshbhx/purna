# Report a problem

> **For anything urgent about someone's health, call your local emergency number or their doctor first.** We can't give medical advice.

## Safety concerns

If anything about Purna seemed unsafe, even if nothing happened, please write to [harshbhx@gmail.com](mailto:harshbhx@gmail.com) with "Safety" in the subject. Tell us what happened, when, which level and settings were used, and how the person is now. We read safety reports first and reply within 3 days. Don't include the person's full name or medical records.

## Security and privacy issues

If you find a way to see or change someone else's sessions, notes or links, please email [harshbhx@gmail.com](mailto:harshbhx@gmail.com) rather than posting it publicly. See the [security policy](https://github.com/harshbhx/purna/blob/main/SECURITY.md). To ask about your own data, see [Privacy](#privacy).

## Bugs, accessibility and ideas

- Something broken or hard to use: open an issue at [github.com/harshbhx/purna/issues](https://github.com/harshbhx/purna/issues), or email us.
- Therapists and researchers: we'd love your feedback on the measures, and clinical collaboration is welcome.

Please include what you were doing, what you expected, what happened, and your device and browser if it's about the website.

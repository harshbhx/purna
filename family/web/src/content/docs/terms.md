Plain-language terms for using Purna and Purna Family. By using them, you agree to these terms.

## What Purna is

Purna is an early prototype, built for a hackathon, of a home practice tool for people with left spatial neglect after a stroke. It is offered free, as it is.

## What Purna is not

- **Not a medical device.** It has not been cleared, approved or certified by any regulator anywhere, for example the US FDA, a CE notified body in the EU, the UK's MHRA or India's CDSCO.
- **Not a diagnosis or treatment,** and not a replacement for a doctor or therapist. Don't start, stop or change any medicine, therapy or care because of a Purna result.
- **Not for emergencies.** It doesn't watch the person or call for help. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

## Using it responsibly

- Use Purna only with the agreement of the person practising, and of their doctor or therapist. Follow [Safety](#safety).
- Use it only for adults (18 and over).
- Share doctor links and invite links only with the person's family and care team. Anyone with a link can see that practice.
- Don't try to get into data that isn't yours, overload the service, or get around its limits. If you find a weakness, please [report it](#report) instead.
- You're responsible for what you write in notes. Don't write anything you wouldn't want the family to read.

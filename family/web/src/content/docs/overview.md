# Purna documentation

Purna is a practice tool for people who miss the left side of the world after a stroke (left spatial neglect). It has two parts: a Lens for Snap Spectacles that turns a cancellation exercise into lighting clay diyas on your own dining table, and **Purna Family**, this website, where the family follows along and the therapist sees the numbers.

> **Purna is a practice tool, not a medical device.** It doesn't diagnose, treat or monitor any condition, and no regulator anywhere has reviewed, cleared or approved it. Use it only as part of the plan your doctor or therapist agrees with.

## Who it's for

- Adults (18 and over) with left spatial neglect after a stroke, who can sit at a table for a few minutes and whose doctor or therapist agrees that home practice is right for them.
- Their carers, who set up each session and sit nearby.
- Their therapist, who decides the level and reads the results.

## Who it's not for

- Anyone under 18. Spectacles are made for adults.
- Anyone in the first days after a stroke, or whose condition is changing, unless their care team says it's safe.
- Anyone who has had seizures, or has a serious medical, vision or psychiatric condition, **without first asking their doctor**. See [Safety](#safety).
- As a test to find out whether someone has neglect. Only a clinician can assess that.

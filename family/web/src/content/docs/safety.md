# Safety

Please read this page before the first session, and keep it to hand. Purna is meant to be calm and low-risk, but the people it's for have been through a stroke, so we ask for more care than a game would.

## Emergencies: Purna can't detect a stroke

If you notice **any** of these signs suddenly, stop the session and **call your local emergency number now**: for example 911 in the US and Canada, 999 in the UK, 000 in Australia, 112 in the EU and India (112 also works from most mobile phones in many countries). Don't wait to see if they pass.

| Sign | Look for |
|---|---|
| **B**alance | Sudden loss of balance or coordination |
| **E**yes | Sudden blurred, double or lost vision in one or both eyes |
| **F**ace | One side of the face droops; the smile is uneven |
| **A**rm | One arm is weak or drifts down when both are raised |
| **S**peech | Slurred, strange or no speech |
| **T**ime | Time to call. Note when the signs started |

Purna doesn't watch for any of these, and a session result can never rule a stroke in or out.

## Before you start

- **Talk to the doctor or therapist first.** Ask them whether home practice is right, which level to use, and how often.
- **Seizures:** if the person has ever had a seizure, ask their doctor before using Spectacles. Seizures are a common complication after a stroke: in one large review, about 1 in 10 people developed epilepsy afterwards.
- **Other conditions:** Snap advises asking a doctor before using Spectacles for anyone pregnant, or with a serious medical, vision or psychiatric condition.
- **Medical implants:** keep the glasses at a distance from pacemakers, hearing aids, insulin pumps and other medical devices, and stop if you notice any interference.
- **Age:** adults only. Spectacles are for people 18 and over.
- **Vision:** missing the left side can also come from visual field loss (hemianopia), not only neglect. Purna can't tell them apart; the therapist can.

## Setting up a safe space

- **Sit down.** Use a steady chair, ideally with arms, at a clear table. Never stand or walk with the Lens running.
- **Clear the table** of anything hot, sharp or breakable. **No real lamps, candles or open flames** on or near the table.
- Keep the area around the chair free of things to trip over, and keep pets out of the way.
- A **carer stays in the room** for the whole session.
- Use the glasses where it's comfortably warm, not in direct heat (Snap's operating range is 0–35 °C).

## During a session

- A session is about **four minutes**. Take a break between sessions, and don't use the display for long stretches.
- Turn the head **only as far as is comfortable**. The exercise asks for head turns to the left; it never needs a quick or forced movement.
- The pinch can be done with a hand resting in the lap: nobody needs to reach across the body.

**Stop straight away and take the glasses off if the person feels** dizzy, sick, a headache, eye strain, neck pain, unusually tired, hot or uncomfortable where the glasses touch, or anything else unusual. Rest, and don't start again that day. If it happens again, tell the therapist before the next session.

**If anyone has a seizure, loss of awareness, or eye or muscle twitching,** stop, keep them safe, call your emergency number, and don't use Purna again unless their doctor says it's safe.

## How Purna is designed to be gentle

- **No flashing.** Nothing in the Lens changes brightness more than three times a second, the limit used to protect people at risk of seizures from light. Changes are smooth fades.
- **No failure sounds and no scores during the task.** Wrong pinches only make a quiet tick.
- **Hints point to a side, never to an answer,** so the person isn't rushed.
- **Plain results,** always labelled "Practice result, not a diagnosis."

## What Purna is not

- Not a medical device. It has not been cleared, approved or certified by any regulator anywhere, for example the US FDA, a CE notified body in the EU, the UK's MHRA or India's CDSCO.
- Not a diagnosis, a treatment or a replacement for therapy. Don't change medicines, therapy or care because of a Purna result; talk to the care team.
- Not for emergencies. It doesn't watch the person and can't call for help.

## Report a safety concern

If something about Purna seemed unsafe, even if nothing happened, please tell us: see [Report a problem](#report). For anything urgent about the person's health, call your emergency number or their doctor first.

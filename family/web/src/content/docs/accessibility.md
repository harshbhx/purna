# Accessibility

Purna is for people living with the effects of a stroke, and for their families and therapists, so accessibility is part of the design, not an extra.

## Purna Family (this website)

- **Standard:** we aim for WCAG 2.1 level AA. Every change is checked automatically with axe on every page and dialog, in light and dark mode, on phone and desktop sizes.
- **Keyboard:** everything works with a keyboard. Each page starts with a "Skip to content" link; dialogs return focus to what opened them; tabs move with the arrow keys.
- **Screen readers:** charts and replays have text descriptions; numbers are written out, so colour is never the only signal.
- **Motion:** if your device asks for reduced motion, animations stop and show their final state.
- **Reading:** plain language, sentence case, large touch targets, and no sideways scrolling from 320 px wide phones to large screens. Links in text are underlined.
- **Language:** the family dashboard is available in English and Hindi. More languages are planned.
- **Dark mode** follows your device setting.

## The Lens on Spectacles

- **No reaching:** every diya can be pinched with a ray from a hand resting in the lap, so nobody has to reach across the body with their stronger hand.
- **Look to light** (planned): holding the head on a diya lights it, for people who can't pinch.
- **Hints in sound and light:** the bell from the left always comes with a glow on the left edge, so the hint works for people with hearing loss.
- **Bright, warm visuals** for the see-through display, big targets (5 cm or more), and no small text during the task.
- **No flashing:** nothing changes brightness more than three times a second.
- **No speaking needed:** stroke can affect speech, so Purna never asks for voice.

## Known gaps

- Messages that come from the server, such as some errors, are still in English only.
- The landing page and the therapist view are in English only.
- The Lens hasn't been tried by people with neglect yet, so its accessibility on real glasses is unproven.

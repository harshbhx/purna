Purna

Practice that makes the world whole again.

Purna is a 4-minute daily exercise on SPECS glasses for people who miss the left side after a stroke. Their family sees how it went. Their therapist gets the numbers.

What a session looks like

How it works

1. Practice. They sit at the table with the glasses on and light the diyas. If they stay on the right too long, a temple bell rings from the left and a soft beam sweeps that way. Hints point to the side, never at the answer.

2. See it. The glasses show a replay of their search and a short report. One pinch sends it here.

3. Share it. The family sees progress over days. A 7-day link gives the therapist every number, and room for a note back.

Invite family

Send this link to a brother, sister or carer. Anyone with it can see this practice, so share it only with family.

Delete everything

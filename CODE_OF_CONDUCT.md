# Code of conduct

Purna is built for people at a hard time in their lives: stroke survivors, the families looking after them, and the therapists helping them. We ask everyone who takes part here to bring the same care.

**Be kind and specific.** Critique the work, not the person. Assume good intent, and say what would make something better.

**Respect lived experience.** People may share their own or a relative's stroke. Don't question it, don't diagnose anyone, and don't give medical advice in issues or discussions.

**Keep it private.** Never post anyone's health details, session data, codes, links or keys. If you see them, tell the maintainer so they can be removed.

**No harassment.** No insults, slurs, sexual content, threats, or unwanted attention, in public or in private messages.

If something here makes you uncomfortable, email **harsh@dualmindlab.tech**. Reports are read by the maintainer and kept confidential. Anyone who breaks these rules may be warned, have comments removed, or be blocked from the project.

This code is based on the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.
